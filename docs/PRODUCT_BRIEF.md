# Product brief

An independent, brand-customizable electronics/tools theme for small-to-medium
catalogs. Help shoppers find a product, understand verified specifications,
compare alternatives, check explicit compatibility and purchase on mobile.
Arabic RTL and English LTR are first-class requirements. No store-specific
contacts, currency or branding; no fabricated urgency, reviews or guarantees.

Design hypothesis: a calm technical catalog with large device imagery, clear
price hierarchy, readable specification rows and restrained transitions. This
first branch establishes components, not finished art direction or differentiation.

One polished preset; approximately 12–16 useful sections is a planning target,
not Shopify policy. Defer AI, PWA/apps, loyalty and independent servers.

## Commercial hypothesis, not forecast

As checked 2026-10-25, Shopify's
[theme revenue-share page](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/store/revenue-share)
states no submission fee and 15% share of gross theme revenue. At a hypothetical
$250 price: two sales = $500 gross; $500 × 0.85 = $425 after this share alone.
Three sales = $750 gross / $637.50 after this share alone. Taxes, refunds,
other applicable charges and project/support costs are not included. Profit is
not yet calculable. Verify commercial terms again before launch. There is no
acceptance guarantee, sale, participant or launch date established.

## Five-merchant research protocol (not yet recruited)

Recruit five electronics merchants only after authorization to contact them.
Record catalog size, languages, data source, setup time, errors and help needed.
Ask each to configure one product's specs without editing code; ask a shopper
to locate a suitable product, compare alternatives and add the intended variant.
Ask what supports willingness to pay, what prevents purchase, and which issue
must be fixed before launch. Record verbatim answers with consent; don't invent
success rates. Draft invitation: “Would you test an early electronics theme and
share feedback on product data setup and shopping tasks? This is research, not
a promise of Shopify approval.” No invitations sent.
