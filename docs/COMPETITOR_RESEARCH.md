Initial public listing/documentation review, not hands-on paid-theme testing.
No source, images, copy or layouts copied. Prices are snapshots, not our pricing
validation. Seller conversion/performance claims are not measured evidence.

## Documentation evidence

- [Kalles overview](https://help.the4.co/hc/articles/6/85/653/kalles-theme-overview)
  lists comparison, mobile navigation, filtering and setup/app dependencies.
- [Minimog product information](https://docs.foxecom.com/minimog-theme/collections-and-products/product-page/product-information)
  documents reorderable blocks, comparison controls and metafield setup.
- [Ella changelog](https://halosoft.gitbook.io/ella-documentation/changelog)
  records fixes involving variant media, sidebar cart, currency filters and RTL.
  These are regression-test ideas, not proof that the current version is broken.

## Small review sample; no prevalence claim

Concept's visible October 23, 2026 review praised custom mobile add-to-cart
support. Warehouse's July 1, 2026 review complained about upgrade/maintenance
effort; other visible reviews praised support. Impact's July 20, 2026 review
reported delayed support during setup; other visible reviews were positive.
These are reviewer statements, not independently reproduced defects. Inference:
setup, update guidance and support responsiveness deserve explicit testing.

Review counts, positive-review percentages, lifetime sales and “trending” are
different measures. None establish current monthly sales, conversion gains or
our demand. RTL/comparison are competitive expectations, not an invented gap.
Full public documentation review for Concept/Impact/Warehouse/Empire and a wider
negative-review sample remain pending; this research is intentionally incomplete.
